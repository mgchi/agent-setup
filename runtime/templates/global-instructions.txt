## Local context and collaboration

Use the context-workflow skill at {{HOME}}/.claude/skills/context-workflow/SKILL.md at task entry and completion. An entry hook receipt means entry already ran. The executable is {{HOME}}/.local/bin/agent-context.

Load relevant context and verify mutable facts against their sources. Current instructions and repository rules govern the work. The lead owns edits and validation. Follow the receipt's completion path. With no advisers, screen the outgoing answer locally in the checkpoint. With selected advisers, share only sanitized task, candidate, and evidence text, and use one bounded wait.

Checkpoint supported preferences, decisions or lessons under stable keys. An empty checkpoint is valid. Read required shortened or omitted sources. Refresh after authoritative context changes, then use `agent-context finish --run <id>` to verify the local revision. Follow project preservation and cleanup requirements. The outbox records decisions once. Workers and excluded projects skip orchestration and retain privacy guards.

Sensitive context stays private. Sanitize tool inputs, artifacts, messages, commits, tickets, and PRs. Once a run ID exists, keep disposable task inputs under {{CONTEXT_ROOT}}/local/runs/<run-id>/task-inputs/ and remove only those individual files after capture. Review outgoing content before sending. Hooks cannot guarantee confidentiality; hosted models receive context loaded into their sessions.
