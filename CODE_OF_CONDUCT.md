# Code of Conduct

Help make Agent Setup a useful, welcoming project for contributors of different
backgrounds and experience levels.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique the work and explain your reasoning.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and disagreement made in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Share only information you have permission to publish.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, or repeatedly derail discussions.
- Keep promotional content and unrelated solicitations out of project spaces.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
maintained for this project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads,
or restrict participation when needed. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and its impact.

To report abusive content or behavior, use GitHub's report-abuse controls on the
relevant content or profile. Keep private information out of public reports.
